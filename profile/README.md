# Incufield

We teach AI automation, and we help our students show their work.

## What we do

- **Learn** - hands-on cohorts that teach you to build real AI automations.
- **Build** - every student ships projects, not just exercises.
- **Show** - graduates publish their work in our public showcase.

## Start here

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [showcase](https://github.com/Incufield/showcase) | Student portfolios. Browse the work, or submit your own. |
| [community](https://github.com/Incufield/community) | Ask questions, share wins, find teammates. |
| [awesome-ai-automation](https://github.com/Incufield/awesome-ai-automation) | Curated tools and guides we actually use. |

## For students

1. Build your project in **your own** GitHub account. You keep it forever.
2. Open a pull request to `showcase` to add your entry.
3. We review it, merge it, and it goes live on our site.
